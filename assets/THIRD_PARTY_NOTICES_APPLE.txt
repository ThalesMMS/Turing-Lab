Turing Lab Apple Platform Third-Party Notices
Generated: 2026-04-23

This notice file covers the Apple-platform third-party and forked dependencies
audited for Turing Lab v1.0 release readiness.

The iOS-only `file_picker` media transitive pods `DKImagePickerController`,
`DKPhotoGallery`, `SDWebImage`, and `SwiftyGif` are intentionally excluded
from this shipped-notices file because Turing Lab prunes them from the iOS build
with `Pod::PICKER_MEDIA = false` and `Pod::PICKER_AUDIO = false`. The dependency
choices are defined by the package metadata and the iOS Podfile.

Dependencies covered in this file:
- graphview 1.5.2 (vendored Turing Lab-maintained fork)
- file_picker 8.3.7
- path_provider_foundation 2.4.2
- shared_preferences_foundation 2.5.4
